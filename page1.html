Tengu Stories
Posted on September 12, 2025 — 18:05

Tengu are mountain spirits with red faces and long noses. They test travelers and sometimes train disciplined monks. Their stories emphasize respect for nature and observation of human behavior.

Kappa Notes

Kappa are mischievous water spirits that love cucumbers. Villagers used to leave offerings to appease them. Shows how folklore shaped daily life and local practices.

The Paper Door Ghost
Posted on September 2, 2025 — 12:03

I found an interesting fragment about a household spirit said to appear only when shoji doors are left slightly open. It doesn’t harm anyone — it simply watches. If you close the door completely, some families believed it would knock back from the other side.

A Story About Rain That Whispers
Posted on August 30, 2025 — 21:47

In some mountainous regions, there’s an old belief that if you walk alone on a forest path and the rain suddenly stops, you should never turn around — even if you hear someone whisper your name. Locals say it isn’t a ghost, but something older, mimicking familiar voices to lure travelers off the trail.
